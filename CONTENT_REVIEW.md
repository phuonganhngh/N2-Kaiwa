# Speaking-bank content review

Reviewed all 214 exercises (STT 1–214) as complete question–hint–answer units.

Each exercise was checked for:

1. A coherent, sufficiently specified situation that could occur in conversation or an explicitly identified role-play.
2. English and Japanese questions asking the same thing.
3. A model answer that actually responds to the question without contradicting its premises.
4. Natural Japanese use of the target grammar in a form consistent with the source meaning/example.
5. Short English keyword hints consistent with the model, without a full translated answer.
6. Appropriate register: formal expressions remain in workplace, polite, reflective, or storytelling contexts; strong expressions remain explicit role-plays.

The source workbook, all original grammar labels, meanings, examples, and STTs are unchanged. Source label anomalies are documented rather than silently repaired. This review concerns authored practice material, not edits to the workbook.

The unlisted exercises were retained after the same checks. Multiple forms within a single source entry need not all appear in its one initial exercise.

## Verification scope

Source fidelity and automated data/prompt/browser checks are run separately. Those tests check coverage, data loading, selections, and copying; they do not certify conversational quality. The content checks above were a separate linguistic and scenario review.
